Ramp the fibre up, do not jump

Chia, fenugreek, figs, makhana, oats, seeds, besan and isabgol together is a large increase from where you are now. Full quantities from day one will cause bloating and gas, and that is the most common reason people abandon a plan.

Week 1 — half chia (5 g), no fenugreek in either meal, isabgol every other night.
Week 2 — full chia, 1 g fenugreek, isabgol nightly.
Week 3 onward — full quantities. Keep water at 3.2 litres or the extra fibre works against you.

If the weight is not moving

If the weekly average has not risen by 0.3 kg after two consecutive weeks, add 250 kcal — an extra chilla at dinner, or 2 more idlis at Meal 1. The calorie figure is a starting estimate; the scale is the truth.

If it rises faster than 0.8 kg a week, ease back 200 kcal. That would be mostly fat, and fat gain works against the triglycerides.
